# Additional Public Datasets for OCTH Validation

## HIGH PRIORITY (Highly Significant)

### 1. Pantheon+ Supernovae (2022)
- **Data:** 1701 Type Ia supernovae, z = 0.001 to 2.26
- **Public:** https://pantheonplussh0es.github.io/
- **OCTH Test:** Distance-redshift relation, Psi(z) evolution
- **Why Important:** Direct probe of cosmic expansion history

### 2. Planck 2018 Full CMB Data
- **Data:** Full-sky CMB maps, power spectra, lensing
- **Public:** https://pla.esac.esa.int/
- **OCTH Test:** Mobius topology at large scales, hexagonal patterns
- **Why Important:** Highest precision CMB data available

### 3. SDSS/BOSS Galaxy Clustering
- **Data:** >1 million galaxies, BAO + RSD measurements
- **Public:** https://www.sdss.org/dr18/
- **OCTH Test:** Hexagonal patterns in 3D clustering
- **Why Important:** Largest spectroscopic galaxy survey

### 4. DES Year 3 Full Results
- **Data:** Cosmic shear, galaxy clustering, 3x2pt analysis
- **Public:** https://des.ncsa.illinois.edu/releases
- **OCTH Test:** S8 tension, modified growth
- **Why Important:** Independent WL confirmation of Euclid results

### 5. eROSITA All-Sky Survey (2024)
- **Data:** X-ray selected galaxy clusters
- **Public:** https://erosita.mpe.mpg.de/
- **OCTH Test:** Cluster mass function, growth rate
- **Why Important:** New probe of structure formation

## MEDIUM PRIORITY

### 6. CHIME/FRB Catalog
- **Data:** 500+ Fast Radio Bursts with redshifts
- **Public:** https://www.chime-frb.ca/catalog
- **OCTH Test:** Dispersion measure vs redshift (Psi effects)
- **Why Important:** New cosmological probe

### 7. Fermi-LAT Gamma-Ray Bursts
- **Data:** GRB locations, spectra, timing
- **Public:** https://fermi.gsfc.nasa.gov/
- **OCTH Test:** Time delays, Lorentz invariance (hexagonal effects)
- **Why Important:** Highest energy photons test spacetime

### 8. H0LiCOW/TDCOSMO Strong Lensing
- **Data:** Time-delay cosmography from lensed quasars
- **Public:** Various papers, data releases
- **OCTH Test:** Independent H0 measurement, Psi effects
- **Why Important:** Geometric H0 independent of distance ladder

### 9. BICEP/Keck B-modes
- **Data:** CMB B-mode polarization (tensor modes)
- **Public:** https://bicepkeck.org/
- **OCTH Test:** Primordial gravitational waves, r constraint
- **Why Important:** Tests inflation + hexagonal modifications

### 10. Gaia DR3 Stellar Data
- **Data:** 1.8 billion stars, parallaxes, proper motions
- **Public:** https://gea.esac.esa.int/archive/
- **OCTH Test:** Local distance ladder calibration
- **Why Important:** Anchors cosmic distance scale

## LOWER PRIORITY (But Still Valuable)

### 11. 2MTF Peculiar Velocities
- **Data:** Tully-Fisher distances and peculiar velocities
- **Public:** https://cosmicflows.iap.fr/
- **OCTH Test:** Local velocity field, bulk flows
- **Why Important:** Tests local Psi variations

### 12. SZ Cluster Catalogs (Planck, ACT, SPT)
- **Data:** Sunyaev-Zel'dovich cluster detections
- **Public:** Various archives
- **OCTH Test:** Cluster counts vs redshift
- **Why Important:** Growth of structure probe

### 13. Lyman-alpha Forest (SDSS/eBOSS)
- **Data:** IGM absorption spectra from quasars
- **Public:** SDSS DR16/DR17
- **OCTH Test:** Matter power spectrum at z~2-4
- **Why Important:** High-z structure probe

### 14. Big Bang Nucleosynthesis Constraints
- **Data:** Primordial element abundances (D, He, Li)
- **Public:** Various compilations
- **OCTH Test:** Early universe Psi constraints
- **Why Important:** Tests very early universe

### 15. Cosmic Infrared Background (CIB)
- **Data:** Planck, Herschel CIB maps
- **Public:** Planck Legacy Archive
- **OCTH Test:** Star formation history, hexagonal patterns
- **Why Important:** Unresolved galaxy emission

## RECOMMENDED PRIORITY ORDER

1. **Pantheon+ SNe** - Direct expansion history test

6. **CHIME FRBs** - Novel Psi(z) test
7. **Strong Lensing H0** - Geometric H0 test
